Our story

One friendship, told from both sides

How I met my wife

Every beautiful love story has a beginning, and sometimes, the beginning does not look like love at all. Sometimes it begins with an introduction, a simple friendship, and two people who have no idea that their paths are slowly leading them toward something beautiful.

My story began at university, in Level 300. It was during this period that I first heard about the woman who would eventually become my wife. I was introduced to her through one of my classmates, who happened to be her cousin. We had not yet met face-to-face, but from the little I heard and the conversations we had, I came to know her as someone very friendly, warm, and deeply spiritual.

Interestingly, although we knew each other during my university days, we never actually met in person. Life continued, and eventually I completed university and began my National Service in 2018.

Then came a day that would quietly change the course of our story: Valentine's Day, 2019. She invited me to a programme called “A Day with My Beloved.” Little did I know that accepting that invitation would become one of the most significant moments in our journey. It was the first time I finally met her in person.

From that day, something beautiful began to grow. With every conversation, every encounter, every passing moment, we got to know each other better. What started so simply gradually became something deeper and more meaningful.

Looking back, I realise that the most important moments in life do not always announce themselves. There was no dramatic sign that told me, “This is the woman you will marry.” It was simply a friendship that kept growing, and a connection that became stronger with time.

What began with a university classmate, a cousin, an invitation, and a programme called “A Day with My Beloved” became the beginning of a journey that led me to the woman I now call my wife.

Obeng-Akrofi

From screens to forever

A decade ago, in 2016, my photo appeared on my cousin's status. He was a mutual friend; the photo caught his eye, and he asked for my number. That single message set everything into motion.

For three years, our relationship lived in the space between screens. Countless hours vanished into voice notes and video calls: a steady rhythm of shared secrets, late-night laughter, and growing closer miles apart.

It wasn't until 2019, when I invited him to a church programme in Kumasi, that we finally stood in the same room. Meeting in person didn't feel like meeting a stranger; it felt like stepping into a life we'd already spent years building together.

Neither of us can pinpoint the exact moment friendship turned into forever. There was no grand speech or sudden shift, just two people continuously choosing each other, falling deeper into a connection that was effortlessly real and mutual.

If someone had told me ten years ago that my coolest friend would one day become my husband, I wouldn't have believed them. Yet here we are, sealing a bond built on patience, faith, and time. God made a way for us, and I am blessed to love him more with every passing day.

Priscilla
